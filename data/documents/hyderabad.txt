Hyderabad is the capital city of the South Indian state of Telangana. Located along the banks of the Musi River, Hyderabad has an estimated population of over 10 million people, making it one of the largest metropolitan regions in India.

The city is historically renowned as the "City of Pearls" due to its historic role in pearl and diamond trading. Historic landmarks include the iconic Charminar, the Golconda Fort, and the Qutb Shahi Tombs.

In modern times, Hyderabad has emerged as a global technology and business hub, often referred to as "Cyberabad". Major multinational technology companies, including Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, and Apple, have established major campuses in HITEC City and Gachibowli. The city is also a leading hub for biotechnology and pharmaceutical manufacturing in Asia.
