Renewable energy is energy derived from natural resources that replenish themselves faster than they are consumed. Primary sources of renewable energy include solar, wind, hydroelectric, geothermal, and biomass energy.

Solar energy uses photovoltaic cells or concentrated solar power systems to convert sunlight directly into electricity. Modern solar farms can generate gigawatts of clean electricity, reducing reliance on fossil fuels.

Wind energy utilizes wind turbines to harness kinetic energy from air currents. Off-shore and on-shore wind installations provide substantial power grid capacity worldwide.

The primary benefits of renewable energy include significant reductions in greenhouse gas emissions, enhanced energy independence, long-term cost stability, and sustainable economic growth. Battery energy storage systems (BESS) play a critical role in balancing intermittent renewable power generation.
